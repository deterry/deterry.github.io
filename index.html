I'm Jose, a senior at Occidental College studying computer science and economics, with a minor in education. I grew up in Boyle Heights and East Los Angeles, and I'm bilingual in English and Spanish.

My research looks at how AI can support students who are often served last: multilingual learners, students with disabilities, and kids without reliable internet. I'm especially interested in speech and language tools that run offline on the devices students already have. I do this work with Professor Joel Walsh.

I also lead technology and data operations at Ednovate Virtual Academy, where I build the tools staff use across 8 campuses, from attendance automation to bilingual family outreach. I tutor and mentor students there too, mostly multilingual learners and students with IEPs.
