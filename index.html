Most of my work starts with one question: what's getting in the way of students or the people teaching them?

At Ednovate Virtual Academy, where I lead technology and data across 8 campuses, the answer is often hours of manual work. So I build tools that take it off people's plates. One tool replaced about 3,000 attendance entries that staff were typing by hand every week, saving hundreds of hours of administrative overhead.

In research, the answer is often access. Alongside Professor Joel Walsh at Occidental College, I helped develop a lightweight AI model that allows kids to practice ELPAC pronunciation in English, Spanish, and Korean with no internet needed. I co-authored the paper behind it and presented our findings at the Festival of Learning 2026 in Seoul.

I am also a member of the 2025–26 cohort of The Barack Obama Scholars Program at Occidental College, recognized for public interest leadership and community advocacy.
